# Opticks

## The Design of the Work
My Design in this Book is not to explain the Properties of Light by Hypotheses, but to propose and prove them by Reason and Experiments.

## Definition of Light Rays
By the Rays of Light I understand its least Parts, and those as well Successive in the same Lines as Contemporary in several Lines... The least Light or part of Light, which may be stopt alone without the rest of the Light... I call a Ray of Light.

## The Nature of White Light and Color
Whiteness and all grey Colours between white and black, may be compounded of Colours, and the whiteness of the Sun's Light is compounded of all the primary Colours mix'd in a due Proportion.
The Sines of Incidence of the several Rays... are to the Sines of Refraction in given Ratios... The Sun's Light consists of Rays differing in Reflexibility and Refrangibility.

## Notable Queries (Speculations at the end of Book III)
Query 1: Do not Bodies act upon Light at a distance, and by their action bend its Rays; and is not this action strongest at the least distance?
Query 30: Are not gross Bodies and Light convertible into one another, and may not Bodies receive much of their Activity from the Particles of Light which enter their Composition?
Query 31: It seems probable to me, that God in the Beginning form'd Matter in solid, massy, hard, impenetrable, moveable Particles... and that these primitive Particles being Solids, are incomparably harder than any porous Bodies compounded of them; even so very hard, as never to wear or break in pieces.
